## Overview

Across two back-to-back internships at DLL, I worked in two departments. In Insurance, I supported the Marketing & Communications Manager in promoting DLL’s insurance offerings through articles, newsletters, videos and social media posts, while updating communication platforms, supporting internal events and aligning messaging with international teams. In Product Management, I translated complex product information into clear, accessible formats such as educational materials, toolkits, presentations and flowcharts, took ownership of small product improvements, and built relationships across departments.

## My Contributions

### Product Education & Documentation

Built product toolkits for financial products, turning lengthy, complex documents into clear visual presentations in DLL’s branding style.

### Internal Education & Engagement

Worked with the Insurance and Product Management teams to educate employees through e-learnings, classroom trainings, website pages, Viva Engage posts and the Insurance Master Quiz, launched for Insurance Awareness Month.

### Cross-Departmental Collaboration

Edited videos, promoted company-wide initiatives and organised informal intern gatherings to build connections across teams.

## What I Learned

- Gained marketing and communication skills by adapting to a formal environment, tone of voice and content strategies.
- Learned to translate complex product information into structured deliverables by selecting the key points and matching the format to the audience.
- Improved my PowerPoint skills by working within DLL’s branding, moving from Canva to a more corporate, structured design approach.
- Enhanced my communication and networking skills by collaborating with Insurance Managers, Legal Counsels, Risk Specialists, Process Owners, Product Managers and Interns.
- Developed initiative by spotting pain points in internal workflows and proposing new projects, such as the Viva Engage content series and the Insurance Master Quiz.
- Strengthened my planning skills by managing several projects at once across two departments, from research to final delivery.

## Outcomes & Impact

These internships gave me an overview of corporate life across different departments. I built strong connections in the company, proposed and delivered several self-initiated projects, and created educational materials that are now used in onboarding and trainings across the organisation. Senior managers reached out to me directly for support, and my manager expressed interest in hiring me after graduation.
