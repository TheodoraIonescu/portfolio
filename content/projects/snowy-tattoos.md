## Overview

Developed a tailored marketing plan and social media content strategy for Tattoo Design Snowy, a new boutique tattoo shop in Breda. The goal was to increase visibility and attract niche clientele through consistent, engaging posts and strategic campaigns.

## My Contributions

### Marketing Plan Development

Collaborated with the team to design a full marketing plan tailored to Tattoo Design Snowy’s brand, target audience, and goals.

### Content Strategy & Creation

Developed a content calendar, promotional visuals, and posts aligned with the shop’s soft and personal brand identity.

## Deliverable

Marketing plan and featured social media content, including a TikTok video idea that was implemented for the client.

## What I Learned

Enhanced my skills in strategic social media planning, client collaboration, content creation, and designing campaigns for niche audiences.

## Outcomes & Impact

Successfully delivered an actionable marketing plan and content strategy. The client received creative, branded social media material and a TikTok campaign idea implemented in real-world promotions.
