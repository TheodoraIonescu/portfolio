## Overview

A multidisciplinary magazine project exploring the intersection of vintage fashion and sustainability. Developed over 15 weeks as part of a university Production House collaboration, the project aimed to merge storytelling, aesthetics, and eco-conscious design into one cohesive publication.

## My Contributions

### Feature Writing & Interviews

I researched and wrote several key stories, including a fantasy narrative and two feature interviews—one with a photographer using vintage cameras to create unique aesthetics, and another with a designer transforming materials like McDonald’s bags into fashion pieces.

### Editorial Design & Visual Integration

Using Adobe Photoshop, I contributed to the layout and visual consistency of the magazine, aligning typography and imagery to reinforce the project’s theme of sustainability and retro aesthetics.

## What I Learned

This project deepened my understanding of how storytelling and design intersect to shape an audience’s perception. I learned how to blend narrative voice with visual form, and how collaborative editing enhances creative outcomes.

## Outcomes & Impact

The final magazine successfully captured the essence of sustainability and vintage culture, receiving positive peer and mentor feedback for both writing quality and design cohesion. It also strengthened my skills in multi-disciplinary teamwork.
