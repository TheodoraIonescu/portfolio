BIP: Intercultural Marketing Project

Brief Overview: This project, in collaboration with Yeșar University and Yanmar, involved creating a marketing campaign to boost sales and brand recognition for Yanmar. Working in an international team, the project included an online research phase followed by an in-person strategy development phase in Turkey.

Project Details

  • My Role:

    Project Lead & Marketing Strategist

  • Tools & Skills:

    Marketing Strategy, Cross-Cultural Communication, Leadership, Research, Strategic Thinking

  • Timeline:

    4 Weeks

  • Client/Context:

    Yanmar (Yeșar University BIP)

My Contributions

check_circle Online Research Phase

Worked closely within an international team to conduct the initial online research phase. This involved analyzing the market and brand positioning for our client, Yanmar, to identify key challenges and opportunities.

check_circle In-Person Strategy Development

Traveled to Turkey for the in-person phase, where I stepped up to lead the project due to team difficulties. I guided my team in developing the final marketing campaign strategy to boost sales and brand recognition.

Reflection & Results

lightbulb What I Learned

  • Strengthened my leadership skills since I had to step up and lead the project, since we had difficulties within our team.
  • Developed Strategic Thinking and thinking out of the box in terms of campaign ideas, since we developed something that is not usual.
  • Improved my presentation skills though holding multiple presentations in front of supervisors and our client.
  • Developed my cross-cultural skills through communicating and working with other nationalities.

trending_up Outcomes & Impact

This has been a great learning opportunity since I worked on a hands-on project for a real client. I developed my skills even further and I created strong connections with lecturers and other students.
