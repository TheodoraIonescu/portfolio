Marketing & Product Management Intern at DLL

Brief Overview: Across two back-to-back internships at DLL, I worked in two departments. In Insurance, I supported the Marketing & Communications Manager in promoting DLL’s insurance offerings through articles, newsletters, videos and social media posts, while updating communication platforms, supporting internal events and aligning messaging with international teams. In Product Management, I translated complex product information into clear, accessible formats such as educational materials, toolkits, presentations and flowcharts, took ownership of small product improvements, and built relationships across departments.
