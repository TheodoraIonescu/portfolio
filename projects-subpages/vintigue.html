Vintigue – A Vintage Fashion & Sustainability Magazine

Brief Overview: A multidisciplinary magazine project exploring the intersection of vintage fashion and sustainability. Developed over 15 weeks as part of a university Production House collaboration, the project aimed to merge storytelling, aesthetics, and eco-conscious design into one cohesive publication.

Project Details

  • My Role:

    Writer, Editor, Visual Designer

  • Tools & Skills:

    Adobe Photoshop, Creative Writing, Interviewing, Editorial Design

  • Timeline:

    15 Weeks

  • Client/Context:

    University Production House Project

My Contributions

check_circle Feature Writing & Interviews

I researched and wrote several key stories, including a fantasy narrative and two feature interviews—one with a photographer using vintage cameras to create unique aesthetics, and another with a designer transforming materials like McDonald’s bags into fashion pieces.

check_circle Editorial Design & Visual Integration

Using Adobe Photoshop, I contributed to the layout and visual consistency of the magazine, aligning typography and imagery to reinforce the project’s theme of sustainability and retro aesthetics.

Reflection & Results

lightbulb What I Learned

This project deepened my understanding of how storytelling and design intersect to shape an audience’s perception. I learned how to blend narrative voice with visual form, and how collaborative editing enhances creative outcomes.

trending_up Outcomes & Impact
